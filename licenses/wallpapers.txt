The wallpapers unslot ships in System/Wallpapers.

Changes made for unslot: the space photos were scaled and centre-cropped to 1440x960; the textures had a
centre section cropped and scaled to 1440x960; the pixel art was flattened from its layers and scaled
up by a whole number to 480 px tall, as noted below. All were re-encoded.

Wallpaper credits
=================

Each entry: file, title, credit, licence, source page.
CC0 and public-domain items need no credit. The credit is listed anyway as a courtesy.
CC BY 4.0 items MUST show the credit line exactly as written, wherever the image is shown or distributed.
If an image is changed beyond resizing or cropping, the publisher suggests noting the change after the credit line.
