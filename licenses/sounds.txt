The menu sounds unslot plays, in crates/slot/assets/ui_*.pcm.

Every one is a recording of a real object, cut from a Freesound upload released under CC0. None is
synthesised. Each was made the same way from the source's high-quality preview (128 kbps MP3):
decoded to 48 kHz mono, high-passed at 80 Hz (2nd-order Butterworth, zero phase) to take out rumble
and DC, cut to one take starting 1.5 ms before its transient, faded in over 1 ms and out over the
last 40% of its length, then levelled so the loudest 30 ms sits at the level noted below (never
past -4 dBFS at the peak), and written as headerless signed 16-bit little-endian PCM.
